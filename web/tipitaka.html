The Tipiṭaka is the largest body of Burmese-script text in existence, and its Pali orthography is the hardest test a Myanmar font can take — stacked consonants on every line. Every canonical book is here: pick a basket, choose a featured text or pull any of the 59 books whole off the shelf, turn the pages, change the font mid-verse. စာမျက်နှာများကို လှန်ကြည့်ပါ။

Featured texts: the Mahāsaṅgīti root edition as published by SuttaCentral (public domain), transliterated by this repository's own pali_translit.py (verified 62,729/62,729 words against the parallel edition). Whole books: fetched as you open them from the Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana XML published by the Vipassana Research Institute — with attribution and thanks; nothing is copied into this repository, and each book arrives with VRI's latest corrections. The canon itself is over two thousand years old and belongs to everyone.

Fonts. Every face here was generated by this project's pipeline from stroke skeletons; letterform skeletons derive from SIL Padauk (OFL). The whole canon — 206,072 distinct words — shapes in these fonts with zero missing glyphs; the 1,633-word Pali cover corpus gates every release.
