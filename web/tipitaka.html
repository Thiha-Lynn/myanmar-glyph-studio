ပိဋကတ် သုံးပုံ · the canon, read in your fonts

The Tipiṭaka is the largest body of Burmese-script text in existence, and its Pali orthography is the hardest test a Myanmar font can take — stacked consonants on every line. This shelf holds the canon's most-recited texts, set entirely in typefaces generated from browser sketches by this project's pipeline. Pick a basket, turn the pages, change the font mid-verse. စာမျက်နှာများကို လှန်ကြည့်ပါ။

Text. The Mahāsaṅgīti Tipiṭaka root edition as published by SuttaCentral (public domain), transliterated into Burmese script by this repository's own pali_translit.py — verified word for word against the Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana parallel edition of the Vipassana Research Institute (62,729 of 62,729 aligned words). The canon itself is over two thousand years old and belongs to everyone.

Fonts. Every face here was generated by this project's pipeline from stroke skeletons; letterform skeletons derive from SIL Padauk (OFL). The whole canon — 206,072 distinct words — shapes in these fonts with zero missing glyphs; the 1,633-word Pali cover corpus gates every release.
