Rendering showcase

This font was not drawn in a desktop font editor. Every outline in it was sketched stroke-by-stroke in the browser over a dimmed guide, and the OpenType shaping — the rules that decide which of four shapes a vowel takes, how wide a ရရစ် wrap has to be, where a tone mark lands — was generated from those sketches. Below is what that produces on the clusters that are hard to get right, next to SIL Padauk, the font the outlines were traced over.

Each row carries its own evidence: the glyphs the shaper actually produced, and how far the result sits from the reference in units of a 1000-unit em. Nothing here is judged by eye.
