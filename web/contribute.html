Four invitations that stay open. Take any of them at any time: nothing to claim, nobody to ask, and partial work is welcome on every one. Five letters drawn in a consistent hand is already a real contribution.

Invitation A

The consonants set a font's whole personality. The families here carry one hand — this invitation is for yours. Trace the dimmed guides, press Save, and open a pull request with your project file.

Twelve drawings, an evening's work, a complete contribution. Myanmar digits are tabular — every figure takes the same width — and the build enforces that for you, so draw freely and let the pipeline space them.

Invitation C

Translate the studio ဘာသာပြန်ပါ

The one thing no single maintainer can do. The interface speaks English and Burmese; Shan, Mon and S'gaw Karen are wanted, as are corrections to the Burmese typography wording. A language is one file, and partial translations work — untranslated strings fall back to English, so twenty lines help immediately.

Invitation D

Judge a font by eye ကြည့်ပြီး ဝေဖန်ပါ

Three shaping engines are verified in software, which proves they draw what the font asks for. It does not prove that what the font asks for looks right to someone who reads Burmese. That judgement is the contribution — and it needs no tools beyond your eyes.

No experience with fonts is needed for A, B or D. If you can trace a letter on a phone, or read Burmese and say "that looks wrong", you can contribute something this project cannot produce on its own. The hard parts — OpenType shaping rules, mark positioning, the build — are already automated.

What happens after you send something

  1. Continuous integration builds your font and checks its shaping against 1,486 test clusters and 711 real Burmese words automatically — you do not need to run anything locally.
  2. You get specific feedback, not a silent queue. If a mark sits two units off, the report says which glyph and by how much.
  3. Your name goes in the font's own credits, and fonts are released under the SIL Open Font License — free for anyone, forever.
The full detail for all four lives in CONTRIBUTING.md, which is the canonical version — this page is the short way in. Questions in Burmese are welcome in Discussions.
